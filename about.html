About Discoverly

Find the cool local spots without endless scrolling

Why I Made This

I love traveling and discovering new places. But honestly, it gets tiring having to scroll through TikTok, Instagram, and Facebook events just to find a good local coffee spot or see what activities are happening in the city.

I'm not really interested in the big touristy attractions. I prefer finding places where locals actually go: small cafés, weekend markets, fun events that aren't advertised everywhere.

When I travel, I like feeling like I'm experiencing the place for real, not just visiting the main tourist spots. I want to see what the culture is actually like and do things that locals do.

The problem is that finding these places takes forever when the information is scattered everywhere. So I built Discoverly because I wanted something like this to exist. Now it's all in one place and easier to find.

No more bouncing between apps trying to piece together where to go. No more missing out on the best spots because they're not advertised anywhere.

Just everything in one place so you can plan your trip and actually enjoy traveling instead of spending half your time researching.
